### 🏢 North Stone Systems

At **North Stone Systems**, the idea is simple:
- **Understand** how a business works.
- **Identify** where time, effort, or processes can be improved.
- **Find practical technology solutions** that make the way a business operates simpler and more efficient.

I’m involved across the different sides of building the venture — from understanding business problems and researching potential solutions to experimenting with AI tools, automation workflows, system integrations, and developing ideas that can grow into practical solutions for businesses.
